# Privacy policy for Rummage

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Rummage is a bookmark manager for Chrome. It has no server. It has no account, and it does not collect, sell or share your data: everything it keeps stays in your Chrome profile on your computer.

## What Rummage reads and why

- Your Chrome bookmarks, to search and organise them. Changes go straight to Chrome's own bookmarks.
- Your browsing history, only to count how often and how recently you opened each bookmark. This powers the "Never opened" list and the "Opened 3 days ago" labels. Visits to pages that are not bookmarked are ignored.
- Your open tabs' titles and addresses, only when you use the command bar, so it can offer to switch to a tab you already have open.
- The text of a page you save with the save popup, so search can find what the page said.

## What Rummage stores

In the extension's local storage on your computer:

- tags and notes you add to bookmarks, plus favourites and reminders
- the text of saved pages, if page search is on or you saved the page with the popup
- the result of each link check, if link checks are on
- how often each bookmark was opened
- bookmarks you removed, for 30 days, so you can restore them
- your settings and rules

None of this is sent anywhere. Uninstalling Rummage deletes all of it at once, since Chrome removes an extension's storage along with the extension. Your Chrome bookmarks themselves are untouched by uninstalling.

## Network requests

Apart from loading its fonts (below), Rummage makes no network requests until you turn on page search or link checks. Both are off by default, and Chrome asks for your permission when you turn one on. With them on:

- Rummage visits the addresses of your bookmarks, a few at a time, to read their text or check that they still work. These requests go directly from your browser to each site, without your cookies, the same as a normal page visit.
- For links that no longer work, Rummage asks the Internet Archive's Wayback Machine (archive.org) whether it has a saved copy. Only the bookmark's address is sent. See the Internet Archive's privacy policy at https://archive.org/about/terms.php.

The extension pages load two fonts from Google Fonts. Google receives a normal font request, with no bookmark or history data. See https://policies.google.com/privacy.

No analytics. No tracking. No ads.

## Backups and exports

Scheduled backups and exports are saved to your Downloads folder as files. They do not leave your computer unless you move them.

## Your control

- Turn off page search and link checks in Settings at any time.
- Empty the bin in Rummage to delete removed bookmarks for good.
- Uninstall Rummage to delete everything it stored.
